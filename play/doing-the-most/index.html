Doing the Most

A plate-spinning show for Eric Francksen. Six plates on six poles, and every one is a real part of his life. They wobble and slow, and he taps to give each a spin while a folk band speeds up behind him.

The six plates

  • Dad: James, Henry, and Oscar
  • The House: the Iverson-Johnson House, 1898
  • Cider Camp
  • GGP Executive Producer: Weekly Connect and IGNITE
  • The Day Job: the AI role
  • The Dragon Table: Dragon House Sessions

Then it gets frantic

When there are too many plates for two hands, his people step in. Crystal. Gordon. James, Henry, and Oscar, each in their own colors. A plate that falls gets caught. Nobody loses here.

At the end every plate spins on its own, and the line reads: You don't have to spin them all alone.
